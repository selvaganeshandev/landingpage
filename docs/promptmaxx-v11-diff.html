The six changes that actually matter

Screen counts undersell this. v11 isn't a reskin — it changes how the product measures, charges, and gets sold. These six shifts are the release.

Today
Ask each AI once
One call per prompt per engine. PromptAnalytics is keyed on (prompt, platform) — no room for a second run.
v11
Ask 3–10 times, keep the ledger
3,591 runs across 399 variants, averaging 9 runs each. Variance flagged, confidence shown as dots, every number links back to the raw responses.
Today
Scattered metrics
Visibility, sentiment, share of voice — each on its own page, no single number, no target.
v11
One score, three pillars, twelve measures
Findable · Cited · Chosen. GEO score 0–100 with bands: Absent → Present → Preferred (51–75) → Default (76+). Every measure has a target and an evidence link.
Today
Dashboards only
You see the gap. Working out what to do about it is your problem.
v11
A sequenced plan with projected scores
Visibility Plan sorts every action into Now / Next / Later with owners, due dates and the score projected at each step (46 → 53 → 61 → 68).
Today
Signup required
Nothing to try before creating an account. No public surface.
v11
Free public audit as the front door
URL in → shareable report out, no login. Doubles as a lead pipeline: 312 audits, 38 claimed, 21 became projects.
Today
Seat and invoice billing
Billing page, invoice settings, invoice details.
v11
Credits, and a sharp pricing wedge
Credits meter crawls, scrapes, reports and drafts. LLM calls are free on BYOK — the stated attack on rivals who charge per run. Live burn rate and forecast.
Today
Agency features spread out
Clients, team permissions, project access and billing across four separate pages.
v11
One Settings, built for scale
Eight tabs. 66 projects, 12 seats, per-project credit caps, white-label tab, 22 module-level permissions per role.

Gone, or not carried across

4 to check

These exist in the shipped app but have no home in v11. Some are deliberate consolidation; at least one looks like an oversight.

TodayIn v11Read
Multilingual MonitoringAbsentZero mentions anywhere in the prototype. This is a real capability we ship and one of the few things Pixis has no answer to. Losing it in a redesign would be a mistake — worth confirming it was intentional.
AI CrawlerAbsorbedNo standalone page, but bot access appears as a scored measure ("6 of 6") and the audit does a 200-page crawl. Folded in, not lost — though crawl frequency and discovery history have no home.
Agent AnalyticsAbsorbedPer-engine breakdowns are everywhere in v11, but model performance, behaviour patterns and referral pathways as a dedicated view are gone.
Bulk Content UploadUnclearBarely referenced. The Prompt Generator takes CSV, but bulk content upload has no obvious equivalent.
Detail pages ×6Now modalsMention, Prompt, Prompt Insights, SEO Keyword, Opportunity and Content Gap detail views became modals. Deliberate and fine.
Auth flows ×5N/ALogin, forgot/reset password, session expired, accept invitation — a prototype doesn't need them.
One thing to decide before build

Multilingual is the only genuine capability loss in this diff. Everything else is consolidation. Confirm whether it was dropped on purpose — if not, it needs a home before v11 is treated as the target design.
