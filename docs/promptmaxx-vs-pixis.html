What to take from Pixis

5 things, in order

Nothing here needs new vendors or new science. Four of the five are work we can already do with what's installed.

1

Ask each AI 3 times, not once

AI answers change every time you ask. Asking once and calling it a measurement is the weakest thing in our product. Three runs across our six engines = 18 samples vs their 12 — instantly the best in the category. Also lets us show "how reliable is this result", which nobody sells.

Costs 3× more LLM spend — price it first

2

Build the site health checks

The eight checks above, on a schedule, adding up to one score out of 100. Biggest gap we have, and it makes our GEO numbers better too. Start with AI-bot blocking and site speed — most value, least code.

Crawler libraries already installed Speed data is a free Google API

3

Free "is AI blocked on your site?" checker

One public page, no login. Type a domain, we read its robots.txt and say which AI engines can't see them. Most sites fail this. Pixis hides the same thing behind a sales demo — ours would be free and open.

~2 days Brings in leads

4

Say why they're missing, not just that they're missing

Sort every prompt into four causes: doing fine · competitor took it (needs outside mentions) · nobody owns it yet (open lane, publish first) · missing on one engine only (technical fix). We already hold all this data — it's grouping and wording, not new tracking.

Mostly existing data Turns a dashboard into advice

5

Show the maths

Next to every score, show what it's out of and how many times we asked. Sounds small; it's how Pixis wins methodology arguments in demos. Cheap, and it turns a question we get asked into a reason to trust us.
