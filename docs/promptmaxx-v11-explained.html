Promptmaxx v11 Explained

What is new, why it was added, and how each piece works. Plus the one word that confuses everyone — "engine" — and what our engine actually has to do.

First: the word "engine"

This word is used for three completely different things in our product. Mixing them up makes everything else confusing, so here they are separated.

Meaning 1 · the chatbots
AI engine

The AI chatbots we ask questions to. We use six. These are outside companies — we just send them a question and read the answer.

ChatGPT · Gemini · Claude · Perplexity · Grok · DeepSeek
Meaning 2 · a feature name
Audit Engine

The name of one new screen. It is a free tool: you type a website address, and it gives back a report. "Engine" here is just branding — it does not mean a separate system.

Meaning 3 · our own code
The engine/ folder

Our own background worker. It is the part of our software that does the slow work: asking the six AI engines, reading their answers, crawling websites, and saving results. The website you click on is fast because the engine does the heavy work behind the scenes.

Our code → asks → the six AI engines
Simple way to remember

AI engines are outside. Our engine is inside. Audit Engine is just a feature name.

What our engine does — 5 steps

Every time we check one question, our engine runs these five steps in order. This is the heart of the whole product.

1 · Ask Send the question to all six AI engines. Ask each one several times, not once — because AI gives a different answer each time you ask.
2 · Read Read every answer. Find: was our brand named? In what position? Was our website linked? How were we described?
3 · Weigh Turn all of that into one score out of 100, using fixed weights: Placement 35% · Frequency 25% · Sourcing 25% · Framing 15%.
4 · Compare Note every other brand the AI named, and match them to the competitors the customer tracks.
5 · Explain Say what changed since last time, why, and the two or three things worth doing about it.
Real example from the prototype: one question, 12 runs across 4 AI engines → score 72/100 (Placement 28.0 + Frequency 19.5 + Sourcing 15.0 + Framing 9.5), up 5 points from the previous batch.

What is newly added

Twelve new screens. Here is each one: what it is, why it was added, and how it works.

1 · Audit Engine

Biggest addition
WhatA free public tool. Someone types a website address and gets a full report. No login, no payment.
WhyToday nobody can try Promptmaxx without signing up. This lets strangers see their own score in 3 minutes — and it quietly becomes our sales list. In the demo: 312 audits ran, 38 people claimed theirs, 21 became paying projects.
HowSix stages, about 3 minutes:
  1. Profile — detect the brand, industry, and who its competitors are
  2. Prompts — write 24 buyer questions automatically
  3. Engines — ask all 6 AI engines (24 × 6 = 144 answers)
  4. SERP + crawl — check 50 Google keywords, crawl 200 pages
  5. Score — calculate the GEO score and SEO score
  6. Publish — create a shareable public link

2 · Runs

Most important fix
WhatA full record of every single time we asked an AI anything. One row per question, per AI, per repeat.
WhyAI answers change every time you ask. Today we ask once and report that as fact — which can be wrong. Asking many times and reporting the average is honest. It also lets us prove our numbers instead of asking customers to trust us.
HowEach question is asked 3 to 10 times on each AI. Every run is saved with its answer, position and speed. If the answers disagree, the row is flagged "variance". A confidence meter (●●●○) shows how reliable each number is.
Why it matters, in one line: ChatGPT said yes on run 1, yes on run 2, no on run 3. Ask once and you might report 100% or 0%. The truth is 67%.

3 · Visibility Health

The scoring system
WhatOne score out of 100, built from 3 questions and 12 measurements.
WhyToday our numbers are spread across many pages with no single answer to "am I doing well?" Customers want one number, and a target to aim at.
HowThree pillars, four measures each:
  • Findable — can the AI reach and read your site? (bot access, page structure, freshness, author signals)
  • Cited — does the AI use your site as its source? (your citation share, competitor share, source age, wrong-info risk)
  • Chosen — does the AI actually recommend you? (mention rate, position, engine coverage, on-brand wording)
Each measure has a target. Anything failing turns red, and red measures automatically become the plan.
Score bands: Absent → Present → Preferred (51–75) → Default (76+)

4 · Visibility Plan

WhatA to-do list of fixes, in order, with the score you should reach after each one.
WhyA dashboard shows the problem but not the answer. Customers kept asking "so what do I do?" This answers it.
HowTasks are sorted into Now / Next / Later, each with an owner, a due date and points gained. The demo shows 46 → 53 → 61 → 68. The list is generated automatically from the red measures in Visibility Health.

5 · Brand Context

WhatOne place to describe the customer's business: who they sell to, how they sell, their price range, their buyer types, and their own documents.
WhyRight now our AI writes generic questions and generic content because it does not know the business. Give it context once and everything downstream gets better.
HowYou fill in business type, sales style, ticket size and buyer personas, and upload documents (PDFs, Word files) which get indexed. Every prompt, gap and content suggestion is then generated against this. It is versioned — you can compare and restore old versions, and it warns you which groups a change will affect.

6 · Brand Narrative

WhatNot whether AI mentions you — how it describes you.
WhyBeing mentioned badly is worse than not being mentioned. If AI keeps saying "stricter eligibility than most", that costs sales even though the mention counts look fine.
HowIt compares what Brand Context says you want to be known for against what the AI actually says. Each line is marked Aligned, Contradiction or Absent. Contradictions can be turned into corrective content with one click.

7 · AI Visibility Report

WhatA client-ready report written in sentences, not charts.
WhyAgencies need something to hand a CMO. Screenshots of dashboards do not work in a board meeting.
HowSections read like a document — "Present, not yet Preferred", "Can the engines reach and read you?", "Now, Next, Later". Every report ends with a methodology note stating exactly which AI models were used, on what date, how many times, and what the limits are.

8 · Credits & Usage

WhatA meter showing what each action costs and what is left.
WhyAsking each question 3–10 times instead of once costs much more. Without a meter, costs run away. It is also a pricing weapon.
HowFixed prices per action, with live burn rate and "runs out in 19 days" forecasting. Per-project caps and auto-pause guardrails.
Group run, all AI engines — 25
Retry one AI engine — 8
Generate variants — 5
Content generation — 40
AI Visibility Report — 100
The clever part: because customers connect their own AI keys, the AI calls cost us nothing — so LLM calls are free. Credits only meter crawls, scrapes, reports and drafts. Competitors charge per run because they pay per run. We do not.

9 · Schedules

WhatSet questions to re-run automatically — daily, weekly or monthly.
WhyAI answers drift over time. Checking once tells you nothing about the trend. And customers should not have to remember to press a button.
HowChoose the group, how often, which AI engines, and how many repeats. The screen shows a weekly credit forecast and warns when you are over budget — the demo shows "4.2× over allowance" with one-click fixes to bring it down.

10 · Connections

WhatOne page for everything we read from or write to.
WhySettings were scattered. Also, connections are a sales argument: we need fewer than rivals do, because we own our own SERP and keyword data.
HowGoogle Search Console, Google Analytics, WordPress, Slack, and the customer's own six AI keys. Plus three developer things that are new for us: an MCP server (query our data from Claude Desktop or Cursor), webhooks, and a public API.

11 · Prompt Generator

WhatFour ways to create the buyer questions we track.
WhyThe whole product depends on tracking the right questions. If the questions are wrong, every number after them is wrong.
HowGenerate with AI · read your own website pages and write the questions back · start from known content gaps · upload a spreadsheet. All four end in a review step before anything is tracked.

12 · What's New

WhatAn in-app changelog where users vote on what to build next.
WhyCustomers do not notice features they are not told about, and voting tells us what to build.
HowA list of shipped and planned items with an upvote button on each.

What our engine must do for all this

The jobs our background worker needs to handle. Green means it is a new job it cannot do today.

Ask the 6 AI enginesHaveAlready working. Sends the question, gets the answer back.
Read the answerHaveFinds brand names, positions, links and tone.

If you only do one thing

Everything else on this list depends on repeating the ask 3–10 times and saving every run. Visibility Health, the Plan, the confidence meter, the Audit report and the credit meter all read from that record. It is the foundation — build it first.
